Questions

Is the 15-minute assessment really free?

Yes. There’s no payment to request or take this 15-minute assessment.

What do I get?

One practical recommendation for one recurring bottleneck. The free session is a focused conversation, not a full written audit report.

What happens after I submit?

I read your answers. If the request is a fit, I’ll email you to book a time. Submitting this form does not book a slot automatically.

What’s the difference between the free session and the paid audit?

The free session is 15 minutes and one next move. The paid audit is a deeper look and a written report. I’ll share the details on the call if it’s useful — there’s no pressure either way.

Do you stay on as ongoing support?

No. Work is an audit, consulting, or a one-time build handed over. I don’t offer ongoing maintenance.

Not yet on this preview. A limited run of free voice assessments is planned. When those spots open, you’ll leave your contact details first, and each call will be capped at 15 minutes.
